## 4. Description

*Where: 1.0 Prepare for Submission → Description*

```text
Too much on your list and no idea where to start? Do This Now picks one thing for you to do right now, based on your priorities, how much time you have, where you are and how you feel.

HOW IT WORKS
Open the app and answer a few quick questions. How much time do you have? Where are you: at home, on your phone, at your computer, at the office or out and about? Are you focused or multitasking? Is the TV on? If you like, also say whether you feel like sitting or moving, and whether you want to get outside or stay cozy.

Tap Tell me what to do, and you get one answer: Do this. The app shows why it picked that task, such as a deadline coming up or time left on a weekly goal. Tap Start to run a timer. Pause it, add 15 minutes, and finish when you're done. Not feeling it? Tap Not now to get a reminder later, or to see something else. Already did it? Say when, and the app takes it from there.

YOUR PRIORITIES
Add one-time tasks, recurring goals (like 3 hours a week, in blocks of at least 30 minutes) and errands. Drag them between four priority levels. Give a task a deadline and say how mad you'll be if it slips. Mark what needs focus, what works while multitasking, what gets you outside and what you can do in the same room as the TV.

Errands that go together get bundled into one trip, and small tasks can be cleared together in one block. When a task keeps stalling, the app checks in and asks whether to loosen the goal, push you on it, lower its priority or let it go. When computer work piles up, it can suggest taking it to a coffee shop or library.

PLAN YOUR DAY
The Day view lays out your day the way things really behave. Work hours are a rock that nothing moves. Tasks are soft and flow around everything else. Events have a solid core you drag to choose when you arrive, plus extra time that can be squeezed. Sleep moves too, but it complains. Make something a must-do and see what it bumps, see what won't fit, and pick a quicker dinner to win time back.

FEATURES
• One clear suggestion at a time, with the reasons behind it
• A timer that sends a notification when your time is up, even with the phone locked
• Reminders for anything you put off
• One-time tasks, weekly goals and errands
• Four priority levels you can drag between
• Swipe a task to mark it done or let it go
• A day planner for work, events, tasks, dinner and sleep
• Example tasks, so you can try it before adding your own
• Light and dark mode

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
There's no account to create and there are no ads. Your tasks are saved only on your iPhone, and nothing is sent anywhere.
```

## 8. App Privacy (the "nutrition label")

*Where: App Privacy → Get Started*

**Answer:** "No, we do not collect data from this app." Click **Save**, then **Publish** if the button appears.

**Why:** Apple says data is "collected" only when it leaves the phone in a way that lets you or a partner access it, and that "data that is processed only on device is not 'collected'." Do This Now saves your tasks, events and settings in a file on the iPhone. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no other company's code, and it never connects to the internet. The timer and reminder notifications are scheduled on the phone itself. The app's Settings screen already tells people "Nothing is sent anywhere," and its privacy manifest declares that no data is collected, so all three agree.

If you ever add iCloud sync (the web version had a sync indicator), sign-in, analytics or crash reporting from another company, this answer has to change.

## 11. TestFlight "What to Test"

*Where: TestFlight tab → click the build → What to Test*

```text
Thanks for testing Do This Now! Start by tapping "Try it with example tasks" on the first screen, so there's something to play with. Allow notifications when asked.

NOW TAB
1. Answer the questions (time, place, focused or multitasking, TV), then tap Tell me what to do.
2. On the "Do this." screen, tap Not now and choose 15 min. Leave the app, and a reminder notification should arrive about 15 minutes later. Also try "Just show me something else".
3. Get a new pick and tap Already done, then choose when you did it.
4. Get another pick and tap Start. Try Pause, Resume and +15 min, then Finished it.

TASKS TAB
5. Move tasks between priority levels: tap Reorder, or touch and hold a task, then drag it.
6. Swipe a task right to mark it done, and another left to let it go.
7. Tap a task to edit it, change something and save.
8. If a check-in card appears, try one of its buttons.

DAY TAB
9. Find the example soccer game (it's set for this evening). Drag its solid purple part to change when you arrive, and watch the rest of the day move.
10. Tap a task on the timeline and choose "Must do today". Keep doing this with more tasks until bedtime gets pushed. Sleep should complain in a bubble on the timeline, a "Bedtime pushed" card should appear, and you should hear a groan if your ringer is on.
11. Try the dinner options under "Get time back".

LOCKED-PHONE TEST
12. Add a task that takes 5 minutes (the shortest), set it to Top priority, then tap Tell me what to do. If it isn't picked, tap Not now, then "Just show me something else" until it is. Tap Start, lock your phone and wait. A "Time's up" notification should arrive after 5 minutes.

When you're done, Settings (top of the screen) can remove the examples or erase everything.

Please let me know about:
- anything that crashes or freezes
- a pick that seems wrong for the answers you gave
- a timer or notification that's off
- text that's cut off or hard to read, in light or dark mode
- anything that's confusing
```
