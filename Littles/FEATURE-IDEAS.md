# Littles: research and feature ideas

Notes from looking at the apps the family uses or is considering (November 2026). The App Store pages couldn't be opened from the cloud session, so this is based on web search results and general knowledge of each app. Check details against the apps themselves.

## The apps

| App | What it's for | What Littles took from it (v1) | Left for later |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Solid Starts** | First-foods database (400+ foods): how to serve by age, choking and allergen notes. Paid tier adds a 100-foods plan, allergen introduction, meal plans, recipes and a food log. Reviews say logging is clunky. | Its own 63-food guide with 6–8 / 9–11 / 12+ month serving notes, choking level and allergens; "first 100 foods" progress; the nine allergens with a rotation status; daily "what to feed" ideas; foods to hold off on | Photos of each serving size, recipes and weekly meal plans, a grocery list |
| **Baby Tracker – MyBaby** | Feeds (breast, bottle, pump, solids), diapers, sleep, growth, health, with timers, charts and caregiver sync | One-tap logging, sleep and nursing timers (switch side), "since last" tiles, typical feed gap and next feed, day totals, timeline, growth charts | Caregiver sync, charts by week, PDF report for the pediatrician, widgets, Watch |
| **Bomi** | Activity ideas for kids by age and time | Activity library filtered by both kids' ages, minutes, place, energy and skill, with a daily pick | Personalized plans that learn from what was a hit, printable activity sheets |
| **CDC's Milestone Tracker** | Free CDC checklists from 2 months to 5 years, photos and videos, "when to act early", summary for the doctor, checkup reminders | The 2022 checklists (paraphrased), yes / not yet / not sure per item, "bring up at the next checkup" | A shareable summary for the doctor, well-visit reminders by age, the "act early" guidance |
| **The Happy Child** | Daily parenting lessons, scripts and courses | Tip of the day and "instead of / try / why" cards | A short weekly course, reminders to try one tip |
| **How to Talk** (Faber & Mazlish style) | Communication skills: acknowledge feelings, describe, give information, one word, write a note, problem-solve | Scripts for meltdowns, not listening, siblings, transitions | Practice scenarios, notes on what worked for each kid |
| **The Wonder Weeks** | Predicts fussy "leaps" by due date, with forum and videos | Nothing (the leap model is the company's own and debated) | A simple "fussy days" log to spot your own patterns |
| **Things to Do with Kids** | Places and events to take kids | Outings like the library and playground in the activity list | Nearby places and events (MapKit, library story times), saved favorites |

Other apps worth knowing about:

- **Huckleberry**: sleep schedules and "SweetSpot" nap timing predictions. Idea: predict the next nap from wake windows by age.
- **Nara Baby**: free, ad-free, strong multi-child and caregiver sync. Idea: the bar for sharing.
- **Baby Connect**: a dozen trackers including bath, milk stash and moods, with reports.
- **Brightwheel / Tadpoles / Procare**: what preschools use to send naps, meals, potty and photos home. Idea: paste or forward the daily report into Littles.
- **Cozi / FamilyWall / TimeTree**: family calendars and lists. Idea: two-way sync with a shared family calendar.
- **Lovevery / Kinedu**: development activities by week of age. Idea: tie activities to the milestones each kid is working on.
- **Tinybeans**: private photo journal shared with grandparents. Idea: a photo on any log entry, and a monthly "firsts" recap.

## Feature ideas, by value

**Next (big wins for two parents)**

1. **Share with both parents and caregivers** through iCloud, so whoever does the bottle logs it, and a sitter or grandparent can be invited for a week.
2. **Widgets and a Live Activity**: "Last bottle 2h 10m ago", the sleep timer on the Lock Screen, and Start buttons (the Do This Now widget code is a model).
3. **Siri and Shortcuts**: "Log a 5-ounce bottle for Griffin", "Griffin's asleep", "When did Blake last poop?"
4. **Pediatrician report**: a PDF of the last 2 weeks (feeds, diapers, sleep, foods with reactions, growth, milestones marked "not yet") to share at checkups.

**Food**

5. Weekly meal planner that keeps allergens in rotation and fits what Blake (preschooler) eats too, with a grocery list.
6. Recipes that cover several first foods at once (egg and banana pancakes, salmon cakes).
7. A "family dinner" view: how to adapt tonight's dinner for each kid.
8. Picky-eater tracker for the preschooler: new-food exposures, safe foods, a "one bite" chart.

**Sleep and routines**

9. Nap predictions from wake windows by age, and a bedtime routine checklist.
10. Bedtime and morning routine charts for Blake (pictures, a sticker at the end).

**Preschool**

11. Import the preschool calendar (ICS link or photo of the paper calendar) to fill no-school days and special days.
12. Paste the daily report from Brightwheel or the teacher's email into the day's log.
13. Teacher and classmate contacts, birthday party RSVPs, and a "what to buy" list for class parties.
14. Morning countdown for school days: "Leave in 15 minutes: shoes, water bottle, library book."

**Health**

15. Vaccine record and checkup schedule by age (AAP/CDC schedule) with reminders.
16. Medicine timer: "Ibuprofen OK again at 2:40 PM", with dose by weight.
17. Sick log: temperature chart, symptoms, when to call the doctor.
18. Teeth: first tooth, brushing streak, dentist visits.

**Play and growth**

19. Activities matched to the milestones each kid is working on ("Blake is working on hopping on one foot: try hopscotch").
20. Nearby outings: parks, splash pads, library story times, with weather.
21. Screen-time and outside-time log, with a weekly total.
22. Potty training mode: timer reminders, a sticker chart, streaks.

**Memories**

23. Photos on any entry, a "firsts" timeline (first food, first steps, first word) and a monthly recap to send to grandparents.
24. Quotes: the funny things Blake says, saved by date.
25. Height and weight percentiles on the growth charts (WHO under 2, CDC over 2).

**Polish**

26. Apple Watch: one-tap bottle, diaper and sleep.
27. Night mode: dim red theme for 3 AM feeds.
28. Units: ml and kg/cm.
