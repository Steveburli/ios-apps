# Fishers Guide

A hands-free, GPS-triggered driving tour of Fishers, Noblesville, Carmel and the White River valley.

- **Stories that find you.** As you drive, the app starts the story for whatever you're approaching. It uses your position, your direction of travel and your speed, and tells you which side of the road the place is on.
- **Direction-aware.** A stop can play only for one direction of travel, or tell a different story northbound than southbound. Places you've just passed don't start talking.
- **"Coming up" teasers** half a mile to a mile before a stop, and a town welcome as you enter Fishers, Noblesville or Carmel.
- **Hands-free.** Music and podcasts duck while a story plays. It keeps going with the screen locked, and the Lock Screen and car controls pause or skip.
- **Offline.** Every story is on the phone.
- **Demo drive.** A pretend car drives the route so you can hear how it works from the couch.

12 stops to start: Fishers City Hall and the Nickel Plate District, Ritchey Woods, Conner Prairie, the Hamilton County Courthouse Square, Potter's Covered Bridge, Strawtown Koteewi Park, Ruoff Music Center, Geist Reservoir, the Palladium, plus town welcomes for Fishers, Noblesville and Carmel.

See `CLAUDE.md` for how it's built and how to add stops.
